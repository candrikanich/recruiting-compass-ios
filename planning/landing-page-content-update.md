### 4. How It Works (3 steps)

**Step 1: Set Up Your Profile**
Tell us your sport, position, graduation year, and key stats. Add your academic info and video links. Takes about 10 minutes.

**Step 2: Build Your School List**
Search and add target schools. The pipeline tracks where you stand with each one — from researching to committed. We pull in College Scorecard data so you can compare schools on the facts.

**Step 3: Start Reaching Out**
Use built-in templates to contact coaches. Log every interaction. Your dashboard shows what needs attention — which coaches haven't replied, what deadlines are coming, and what to do next.

### 8. FAQ Section

**Q: Is The Recruiting Compass free?**
A: Yes. The core platform is free for student athletes and their families.

**Q: What sports do you support?**
A: 19 sports across all NCAA divisions — baseball, softball, basketball, football, soccer, volleyball, lacrosse, field hockey, ice hockey, tennis, golf, swimming & diving, track & field, gymnastics, and beach volleyball. Each sport has its own recruiting calendar, metrics, and compliance rules.

**Q: Do I need to hire a recruiting service?**
A: No. The Recruiting Compass gives you the tools to manage recruiting yourself. You'll know what to do, when to do it, and how to reach out to coaches — without paying thousands for a service that does the same thing.

**Q: Can my parents see my recruiting info?**
A: Yes. Family accounts let parents and players share the same dashboard. Parents get full visibility into schools, coaches, interactions, and timelines. The player controls the profile.

**Q: How do the recruiting calendars work?**
A: We built 22 NCAA recruiting calendars from official NCAA PDFs. Select your sport, gender, and division, and you'll see exactly when contact periods, quiet periods, and dead periods are — so you know when coaches can reach out and when you should.

**Q: What are the communication templates?**
A: 33+ pre-written email and text templates for every stage of recruiting — introductions, follow-ups, campus visit requests, thank-you notes, and more. They auto-fill your name, sport, stats, and school info. Built-in NCAA compliance checks prevent you from sending during restricted periods.

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Your recruiting data is private to you and your family. Your public profile is only visible when you share the link — it's not indexed or searchable. We don't sell your data.

**Q: What's the difference between the web app and the iOS app?**
A: Both have the same features and share the same data. Use whichever is more convenient — or both. Changes sync automatically.

**Q: I'm a freshman. Is it too early to start?**
A: No. The recruiting timeline starts in 9th grade. The earlier you begin tracking schools and building your profile, the more prepared you'll be when coaches start paying attention. Most families wish they'd started sooner.

**Q: How is this different from other recruiting platforms?**
A: Most platforms are either expensive recruiting services that do the work for you, or simple spreadsheets. The Recruiting Compass is a management tool — it gives you the structure, templates, calendars, and tracking to run your own recruiting process effectively. You stay in control.

### Content principles applied
- **Specificity over vagueness:** "33+ templates" not "communication tools"; "22 NCAA calendars" not "stay compliant"; "5-stage pipeline" not "track schools."
- **Numbers build credibility:** 19 sports, 66+ metrics, 33+ templates, 22 calendars, 11 action-item rules.
- **Dual audience:** Parents and players have different anxieties. Address both.
- **Anti-recruiting-service positioning:** "No recruiting service required" is the key differentiator. Families spend $2K-$10K on services. This is free.
- **Action-oriented:** Every section ends with what the user gets to do, not what the product is.
