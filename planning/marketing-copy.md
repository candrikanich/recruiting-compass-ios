## 2. App Store Copy

### Subtitle (30 words max)
**College recruiting tracker for 19 sports — manage schools, coaches, timelines, and outreach in one place. Built for athletes and parents.**

### Long Description

**Finally, a recruiting app built for your sport — not just baseball.**

The Recruiting Compass is the only recruiting platform that gives high school athletes AND their parents a shared command center for the entire college recruiting journey — from freshman year through signing day, across 19 sports.

**TRACK EVERY SCHOOL**
Build your college list with real data. Every school is enriched with College Scorecard stats — graduation rates, enrollment, costs — so you're comparing apples to apples. Move schools through a 5-stage pipeline (Researching → Contacted → Visiting → Offer Received → Committed) and see your funnel at a glance. Personal Fit signals score each school on location, campus size, and cost so you focus on schools that actually fit.

**MANAGE EVERY COACH RELATIONSHIP**
A full CRM for your recruiting contacts. Track response rates, see days since last interaction, log every call, email, and campus visit. Coach Insights surface who's engaged and who's gone quiet — so you follow up at the right time, not too early, not too late.

**SEND SMARTER OUTREACH**
33+ proven outreach templates with smart variable resolution — your stats, your highlights, your school preferences drop right in. NCAA compliance guardrails keep your communication within contact rules. One tap to email or text, with engagement tracking built in.

**KNOW WHAT TO DO NEXT**
A 4-year recruiting timeline breaks the journey into phases with age-appropriate tasks and guidance. The AI-driven Action Items engine surfaces exactly what to do today — update your highlight reel, follow up with that D1 coach, register for an ID camp. No more guessing.

**NCAA RECRUITING CALENDARS**
22 sport-specific and gender-specific calendars built from official NCAA rules. Know exactly when coaches can contact you, when dead periods hit, and when evaluation windows open — for YOUR sport, YOUR division.

**TRACK YOUR PERFORMANCE**
66+ sport-specific metrics with trend tracking. Log your times, distances, scores, and watch your progress over time. Your verified stats flow directly into outreach templates and your public profile.

**SHARE YOUR PROFILE**
Generate a shareable public athlete page — highlight reel, stats, academics, and contact info in one link. See which coaches opened it and how long they looked.

**BUILT FOR FAMILIES**
Parents and players share one account with role-appropriate views. Parents see the big picture — costs, timelines, school fit. Players see their stats, templates, and coach conversations. Everyone stays on the same page.

**ACCESSIBLE & PRIVATE**
WCAG AA compliant for screen readers and assistive technology. COPPA compliant for athletes under 13. Your data stays yours.

19 sports: Baseball, Softball, Basketball, Football, Soccer, Volleyball, Beach Volleyball, Tennis, Golf, Swimming, Track & Field, Cross Country, Lacrosse, Field Hockey, Wrestling, Gymnastics, Ice Hockey, Rowing, Water Polo.

Download The Recruiting Compass and turn recruiting from overwhelming to organized.

Track every college coach you contact: response rates, days since last interaction, sentiment, and communication history. 33+ outreach templates built for real recruiting scenarios — initial introduction, post-showcase follow-up, campus visit request, offer response. Move every college on your list through a visual pipeline: Researching → Contacted → Visiting → Offer Received → Committed. See your recruiting funnel at a glance. Every school is enriched with College Scorecard data — graduation rates, enrollment, costs, acceptance rates — so you compare programs with real numbers, not vibes.

### 6. Personal Fit Signals
Not every school is right for every athlete. Personal Fit scores each school on location proximity, campus size preference, and cost against your family's criteria. Filter your list to schools that fit your life — not just your sport — before you invest time in outreach.

### 7. 4-Year Recruiting Timeline
Recruiting looks different freshman year vs. junior year. The timeline breaks the journey into phases with age-appropriate tasks, NCAA milestones, and actionable guidance. An AI-driven Action Items engine surfaces exactly what to do today — update your highlight reel, register for that camp, follow up with Coach Smith.

### 8. Performance Tracking
66+ sport-specific metrics with trend tracking and formatting conventions for each sport. Log your times, distances, averages, and watch your trajectory over time. Your verified metrics flow directly into outreach templates and your public profile — coaches see real numbers, not self-reported claims.

### 9. Shareable Public Profile
Generate a polished public athlete page — your highlight reel, verified stats, academic info, and contact details in one shareable link. Track which coaches opened your profile, how long they looked, and when they came back. Know who's interested before they tell you.

### 10. Family Command Center
Parents and athletes share one account with role-appropriate dashboards. Parents see school costs, fit signals, and the recruiting timeline. Athletes see their stats, outreach queue, and coach conversations. Everyone sees the same pipeline. Recruiting is a family effort — your tool should reflect that.

### 11. Analytics Dashboard
Charts, funnels, and trend lines that turn your recruiting activity into actionable data. See your outreach volume, response rates, school pipeline velocity, and coach engagement patterns. Know whether your effort is converting to results — and where to double down.

### 12. Push Notifications & Compliance
Real-time alerts when coaches engage with your profile, when follow-up windows open, and when NCAA calendar periods change. WCAG AA accessible for athletes using assistive technology. COPPA compliant for athletes under 13. Your recruiting data stays private and secure.
