Family setup and management are easier, plus a round of fixes.

• No family yet? You'll be asked to create one or join with a Family Code.
• Parents can now leave a family, and Family Management explains when Remove or Leave isn't available.
• Parents can choose whether an email invite is for the player or another parent. Once your family has its player, invite relatives to follow along.
• Family changes now show up in your notifications.
• Clearer birthday errors on your profile, plus updated Help, FAQ and Terms.
• Fixed the public profile editor for parents: it now edits the selected athlete.
• Fixed recommended schools reappearing on your dashboard after you add them.
• Fixed the Schools list missing city and state, and Add Event opening before your family loaded.
• Fixed the signup age check being a day off near a 13th or 18th birthday.
